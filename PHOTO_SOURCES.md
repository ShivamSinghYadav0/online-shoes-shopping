## Other product photography

Images for the remaining sample catalog are linked from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/). Please review the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Photo URLs and source IDs appear in `catalog.js` and keep the work reproducible.

A photographer's image copyright license **does not grant trademark rights** to any Nike, adidas, or other visible logos. Do not publish these sample items as genuine AUVRYKO merchandise or use the photos for actual commercial orders without replacing them with owned/authorized product imagery, real inventory and brand approvals.

## Why assets are remote

The sandbox where this portfolio was authored cannot retrieve and bundle the external photographs. The GitHub project therefore links directly to image providers and shows a styled offline state if an image fails. If production hosting is desired, replace `img` URLs with **your own legitimately licensed images**, save them to `public/assets/products`, and use matching photography for every genuine SKU.

Unlike a computer-rendered 3D shoe, **this site does not claim to produce 360° views or invented rear/sole views**. Real alternate-angle sets are available only where multiple source photographs document the same model.

## V11 viewer behavior
Arrows navigate only actual available photographs of the same sneaker model. The +/-/RESET zoom controls are independent of camera-view switching. Products with one photo do not invent rear/front views.

The featured portfolio item (#1) now uses the multi-photo study so new visitors can immediately try the angle navigation. This is a demonstration association, **not** a genuine AUVRYKO product or accurate inventory model. The previous single source photo is retained for concept item #3.
